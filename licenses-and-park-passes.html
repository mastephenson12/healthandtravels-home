Before you leave

Match the Pass to Your Trip

Different agencies use different passes. Check your exact park or recreation site, and keep any required fishing license separate from your admission or parking pass.

Use the free packing checklistPlan your day with Sage

Check these before buying

  • Which agency manages the exact place you’re visiting?
  • Does your pass cover that site and the activity you plan?
  • Are reservations, timed entry, camping, tours, or other fees separate?
  • Do you qualify for a youth, senior, military, or Access pass option?

Passes do not replace checking closures, weather, or the destination’s current rules. Official sources reviewed October 22, 2026.
