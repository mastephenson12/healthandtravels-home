A free Arizona nature scavenger hunt for kids. Pick a desert or forest list and notice what is already around you. A short walk counts.

Choose our hunt ↓

No signup · No collecting · Phone or printable

Try the five-find version

Choose any five prompts. Let your child point, describe or draw what they notice. You can do this from a bench or an accessible overlook too.

Finding everything is optional. Stop when your family is ready.

How to play

  1. Choose an open, suitable place and check its current conditions and rules.
  2. Pick a list. One adult reads while the kids look, or print a copy before leaving.
  3. Stay together on designated paths. Look and listen; leave discoveries in place.

For younger kids, read one prompt at a time. Older kids can sketch a favorite find or describe what makes two shapes different. Skip anything that is not present or cannot be seen safely.

An adult leads the outing. Do not touch cactus, reach into holes, approach wildlife or move toward an edge or water to finish a prompt. Check weather and bring your usual supplies; use the packing checklists before you go.
