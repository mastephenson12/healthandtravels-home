About the Founder

Mark Stephenson, RN is an Arizona father, registered nurse, and outdoor enthusiast. Health & Travels combines clinical health awareness—including hydration, heat-illness prevention, and safety protocols—with hands-on family trail experience across Arizona.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers about Health & Travels and our planning tools.

What is Health & Travels?

Health & Travels helps families plan Arizona hikes, day trips, and weekend getaways using practical information about heat safety, shade, trail difficulty, bathrooms, water availability, and drive times.

What is Sage by Health & Travels?

Sage is our custom adventure-planning tool located at sage.healthandtravels.com. It helps families filter destinations based on children's ages, weather conditions, shade availability, and stamina levels.

How are your trail safety guidelines verified?

Guides are developed using personal field verification alongside official data from the National Park Service, Arizona State Parks, the USDA Forest Service, and National Weather Service heat advisories.
