Plan Around the Season

Arizona changes dramatically with elevation and season. Planning around that reality is more useful than pretending the whole state has Phoenix weather.

Summer

Head Higher

Favor Rim Country, Flagstaff, Williams, lakes, forests, early starts, shade, and heat-aware travel days.

Fall

Forests & Color

Look toward higher elevations, scenic drives, cooler hiking weather, and family weekends in Arizona’s pine country.

Winter

Desert Season

Phoenix, Tucson, southern Arizona, and lower-elevation trails become much more inviting for family outdoor time.

Spring

Desert + Wildflowers

Use mild temperatures for desert hiking, spring blooms, road trips, and longer outdoor days before serious heat arrives.
