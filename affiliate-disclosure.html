Affiliate Disclosure

Some recommendations may earn Health & Travels a commission without increasing your price.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

When a page contains an affiliate relationship, we aim to disclose it clearly near the recommendation or before affiliate links.

How affiliate links work

If you click a qualifying link and purchase or book something, Health & Travels may receive a commission. You generally pay the same price whether or not the link is affiliated.

Editorial independence

Compensation does not guarantee coverage or a positive review. We try to recommend products, lodging, tours and services because they are relevant to the trip being planned. Readers should independently verify pricing, availability, cancellation terms, safety and suitability.

Potential partners

Affiliate relationships may include travel-booking platforms, tours, lodging, outdoor equipment, books, downloadable products and other family-travel services. Merchants and programs can change over time.

Sponsored content

Paid placements, hosted experiences or sponsored content will be identified as sponsored, paid or provided when applicable.

Amazon notice

If Health & Travels participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, qualifying purchases made through eligible Amazon links may earn a commission.

Questions

Questions about a commercial relationship may be sent to helpme@healthandtravels.com.
