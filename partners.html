Who we’d like to meet

Local guides, outdoor outfitters, independent places to eat or stay, museums, cultural organizations, artisans and community groups. Start with an experience you know well in Phoenix, Sedona, Flagstaff, Payson or another Arizona community.

You don’t need to offer a discount. You do need to describe your offering honestly and be willing to keep its information current.

What partnership means here

  • Useful local knowledge. Specific advice and a real connection to the place.
  • Clear expectations. Upfront costs, booking requirements, availability and cancellation information.
  • Honest family fit. Describe age suitability, effort, accessibility, bathrooms and limitations. “Accessible” should come with details.
  • Responsible operation. Follow applicable operating requirements and land-manager rules. Tell us which permits, qualifications or insurance apply to your activity so we can review them.
  • Care for people and place. Respect visitors, residents, cultural sites and the environment.
  • Information we can maintain. Confirm details before publication and tell us promptly about closures or important changes.

Applying does not award a badge or guarantee publication. Any future “Trusted Partner” label requires a completed review; it is not a medical endorsement or a guarantee of safety.

What you get—and what we’re testing

Selected pilot partners collaborate on an experience profile and can be included in relevant guides. Reviewed experience records will be prepared for Sage; automated partner recommendations are not live yet.

There is no pilot listing fee and no required discount. We cannot promise leads, bookings or revenue. Any later paid content or referral arrangement would be agreed separately and disclosed to travelers. Payment will not buy a trust designation or secretly determine recommendations.

Trips go to the business for booking. The pilot does not collect customer payments or sell bundled travel packages.

Questions before you apply?

Do we have to pay or offer a discount?

No. This pilot has no application or listing fee. We’re testing whether useful local experiences help travelers and participating businesses.

Can we join from another state or country?

The first pilot is in Arizona. You can email an introduction for a future destination, but we are not promising expansion dates.

What happens after we email?

We review the fit, ask follow-up questions where needed, and contact you about a possible next step. Nothing is published automatically.
