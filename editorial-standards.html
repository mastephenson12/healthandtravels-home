Editorial & Verification Standards

How Health & Travels ensures accuracy, clinical safety, and real-world family reliability across all guides and tools.

1. Verification & Field Research Standards

We believe family safety requires clear, unambiguous details. Destination and trail data published across Health & Travels and Sage must meet at least one of these criteria before publication:

  • Personally Verified: Inspected firsthand by founder Mark Stephenson or verified contributors, including real-time checks on shade levels, parking capacity, restroom cleanliness, and kid accessibility.
  • Officially Confirmed: Cross-referenced directly with managing government land agencies, municipal park departments, or regional transit authorities.

2. Health, Heat & Safety Protocols

Outdoor activity in Arizona presents unique environmental risks, particularly for children and older adults. Led by founder Mark Stephenson, RN, our health guidance adheres to clinical safety frameworks:

  • Heat Exposure & Hydration: Hydration guidelines follow recommended intake rates from the National Weather Service and Arizona Department of Health Services.
  • Temperature Thresholds: We explicitly warn against mid-day low-desert hiking when ambient temperatures exceed 100°F (38°C) or when Excessive Heat Warnings are active.
  • Medical Disclaimer: Information provided on Health & Travels is for educational and planning purposes and does not replace professional medical advice.

3. Official Data Sources

We routinely verify trail conditions, fire restrictions, water availability, and park closures against authoritative sources, including:

National Park Service (NPS) USDA Forest Service Arizona State Parks & Trails Bureau of Land Management (BLM) National Weather Service (NWS) AZ Dept of Health Services (ADHS)

4. AI & Technology Policy

Technology tools, including automated data aggregation and natural language models, are used solely to assist with data formatting, search indexing, and structural organization. All destination recommendations, safety advisories, and logistics reviews are personally reviewed and edited by human creators before publication.

5. Corrections & Updates

Outdoor conditions change rapidly due to weather, seasonal shifts, and park maintenance. We systematically review and update our core guides. If you spot an outdated trail detail or facility closure, please let us know so we can update it immediately for other families.
