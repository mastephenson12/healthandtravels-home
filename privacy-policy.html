Privacy Policy

How Health & Travels handles website data, analytics, advertising technologies and communications.

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Health & Travels is operated by Mark Stephenson. Questions about this policy may be sent to helpme@healthandtravels.com.

Information we collect

We may receive information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address when you subscribe, contact us, request a resource or use an interactive trip-planning feature. We may also collect technical information automatically, including IP address, browser and device information, referring pages, pages viewed, approximate location, interactions and cookie identifiers.

How we use information

  • Provide, secure and improve our websites, guides and planning tools.
  • Deliver newsletters or resources you request.
  • Understand site usage and measure content performance.
  • Respond to questions and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • Display, measure and personalize advertising where permitted.

Google services and advertising

We use Google products that may collect information through cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and similar technologies. These services may include Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to our sites and other sites on the internet.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You may manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Additional opt-out choices may be available through AboutAds.

Cookies and consent

Cookies help remember preferences, measure traffic, secure services and support advertising. Where applicable law requires consent, visitors will be offered choices before non-essential cookies are used. Browser controls can also block or delete cookies, although some features may stop working correctly.

Newsletter and service providers

Our newsletter is hosted by Beehiiv. Trip-planning, analytics, hosting, customer-management and payment functions may be provided by services such as Vercel, Google, GoHighLevel, Stripe and other vendors. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Affiliate links

Some links are affiliate links. A merchant may use cookies or similar tracking to attribute a purchase to Health & Travels. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information in the ordinary meaning of selling a customer list. We may share information with service providers, advertising and analytics partners, when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here.

Children’s privacy

Our content helps adults plan family activities and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted information may contact us to request deletion.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in each message. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will appear above.
