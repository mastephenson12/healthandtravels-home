02 / Your checklist

Family day hike

Choose a route for the least-experienced person. A shorter outing with an easy turnaround is a perfectly good adventure.

Before you leave
In your pack
For your family

A packed bag does not make every day a hiking day. Change the plan when heat, storms, closures or your group’s energy make the outing unsuitable. Do not depend on unconfirmed trail water.

02 / Your checklist

First camping weekend

Start with a developed campground and a short stay. This is a car-camping starter list, not a backcountry packing plan.

Book it and check it
A comfortable night
Food, water and family extras

Borrow before you buy. Try a borrowed tent at home and check that all its parts are present. Follow campground rules for food storage; a cooler alone may not meet them.

02 / Your checklist

Arizona road trip

Keep the driving realistic, plan rest stops and leave room for a change of plans. For a trail stop, take the hiking essentials too.

Before the drive
Keep these within reach

A trail stop is still a hike. Leave the car with your hiking supplies, even when the walk looks short. Review the day-hike list before you set off.
