# SOP 001 — Health & Travels → Sage → Arizona Hikers Association

## Purpose
Create a consistent customer journey that moves Arizona families from discovering useful information to planning an adventure, joining the community, and returning for future trips.

## Trigger
Use this SOP whenever a new page, feature, campaign, lead magnet, newsletter, Sage experience, or AHA offer is created or changed.

## Owner
Health & Travels ecosystem operator.

## Inputs
- Audience need
- Content or feature being created
- Desired customer next step
- Relevant Sage destination or planner link
- Relevant AHA membership or community link

## Steps
1. **Attract** through useful Arizona content on Health & Travels.
2. **Help first** by answering the visitor's immediate question clearly.
3. **Move to Sage** when personalized planning would help.
4. **Capture the relationship** through the newsletter or lead magnet when appropriate.
5. **Introduce AHA** when the visitor wants ongoing community, trail ideas, events, or member resources.
6. **Retain** through useful recurring content, community activity, and future adventure planning.

## Brand roles

### Health & Travels
Discovery, education, trust, safety, destination guides, and inspiration.

### Sage
Personalized planning and decision support.

### Arizona Hikers Association
Community, ongoing engagement, events, member resources, and recurring membership value.

## Quality check
Before publishing or launching anything, confirm:
- The visitor receives useful value before a sales ask.
- The next logical step is obvious.
- Sage is linked when planning is relevant.
- AHA is introduced where ongoing community is relevant.
- The page does not force irrelevant CTAs.
- Tracking parameters are present on important cross-site links.

## Output
A customer experience that fits the ecosystem journey:

**Explore → Plan → Join → Return**

## Next step
Measure where users continue, stop, subscribe, plan, or join, then improve the weakest transition.

## Metrics
- Search impressions and clicks
- Page engagement
- Sage clicks
- Newsletter signups
- Sage plan starts/completions
- AHA membership page visits
- Checkout starts
- Membership conversions
- Community participation
- Renewals
