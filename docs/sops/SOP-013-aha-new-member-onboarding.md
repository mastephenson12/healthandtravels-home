# SOP 013 — Arizona Hikers Association New Member Onboarding

## Purpose
Give every new AHA member a clear, welcoming first experience that quickly delivers value and gets them into the community.

## Trigger
Run immediately after a membership purchase is completed.

## Success Standard
Within the first 7 days, the member should:
- receive confirmation
- understand what they joined
- know how to access the community
- know where to start
- receive at least one useful resource
- take at least one participation action

## Step 1 — Purchase Confirmation
Immediately confirm:
- membership was successful
- plan selected: monthly or annual
- support/contact method
- next action to access the community

Avoid generic confirmation pages that say little more than “thanks.” The member just paid money. Reward this radical act of trust with clarity.

## Step 2 — Welcome Page
The welcome page should include:
- concise welcome message
- community access/join button
- what to do first
- key member benefits
- link to current trail resources
- link to Sage where useful
- support contact

## Step 3 — Community Access
Confirm the member can actually enter the GoHighLevel community.

Test the invite/access process periodically using a real non-admin email because automated invitations have a charming habit of becoming theoretical.

If an invite does not arrive:
1. verify email address
2. check spam/promotions
3. resend through approved workflow
4. provide the direct supported community-access route if available
5. document recurring failures

## Step 4 — First Member Actions
Encourage one or more low-friction actions:
- introduce yourself
- share your Arizona region
- share favorite trail or outdoor spot
- choose an upcoming adventure
- use Sage to build a plan
- download a member resource

Do not require a massive profile setup before providing value.

## Step 5 — Welcome Sequence
Recommended cadence:

### Message 1 — Immediately
Welcome + access instructions + what to do first.

### Message 2 — Day 1
Show one useful member resource or trail-planning benefit.

### Message 3 — Day 3
Invite participation in a discussion, event, challenge, or trail-planning activity.

### Message 4 — Day 7
Recap available benefits and point the member toward the next relevant action.

Keep messages useful rather than sending four versions of “remember that membership you bought?”

## Step 6 — Segment Member
Store at minimum where technically available:
- monthly vs annual
- join date
- acquisition source
- family/solo interest if voluntarily supplied
- region/location preference if voluntarily supplied
- engagement status

Do not collect information merely because a field exists.

## Step 7 — First-Week Check
Review whether the member:
- accessed community
- opened welcome email
- clicked resources
- participated
- encountered delivery/access problems

For manual early-stage operations, review new-member failures individually so systemic problems become visible quickly.

## Quality Check
Periodically test:
- checkout → welcome redirect
- confirmation email
- community invite
- mobile experience
- all resource links
- unsubscribe/support links
- monthly and annual checkout paths

## Metrics
Track:
- successful onboarding delivery rate
- community-access completion
- first-7-day participation
- first resource click/download
- onboarding email engagement
- support failures
- 30-day retention

## Escalation
If community access, payment confirmation, or email delivery fails repeatedly, stop treating it as an individual-user problem and fix the underlying workflow.

## Next Step
After onboarding, members enter SOP 014 — Community Engagement and Retention.
