# SOP 016 — Affiliate Link Placement

## Purpose
Use affiliate links in a way that helps readers, protects trust, complies with disclosure requirements, and supports Health & Travels revenue without turning useful travel content into a digital yard sale.

## Trigger
Use this SOP whenever adding, reviewing, replacing, or removing affiliate links on Health & Travels, Sage, newsletter content, AHA resources, or downloadable guides.

## Owner
Health & Travels Content / Revenue Operator.

## Core Rule
The recommendation must make sense even if there were no commission.

## Inputs
- Page or content item
- Reader intent
- Product/service being considered
- Affiliate program
- Destination URL
- Disclosure requirements
- Existing competing links

## Placement Priority
Affiliate links should appear only where they naturally help the user complete the trip or solve a problem.

Best-fit categories may include:
- Lodging
- Travel booking
- Outdoor gear
- Family travel gear
- Hiking accessories
- Maps or trip tools
- Relevant local experiences

## Do Not Place Affiliate Links
Avoid affiliate links when:
- The item is irrelevant to the page intent.
- The recommendation is based only on commission rate.
- A safety-critical statement could appear biased by monetization.
- The page is primarily emergency or hazard guidance and the link distracts from the safety message.
- The product/service cannot be reasonably evaluated or described accurately.

## Disclosure Standard
- Maintain a site-wide affiliate disclosure page.
- Add a clear disclosure near monetized content when required or appropriate.
- Use plain language.
- Do not hide disclosure in tiny text, hover states, or vague wording.

Suggested concept:
"Some links may be affiliate links. If you book or buy through them, Health & Travels may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."

## Link Placement Rules
1. Solve the reader's immediate question first.
2. Place the affiliate link near the relevant planning decision.
3. Use descriptive anchor text.
4. Avoid excessive repeated links to the same offer.
5. Do not make every button an affiliate CTA.
6. Preserve a clear path to Sage and AHA where appropriate.
7. Add tracking parameters when supported and useful.

## Destination Guides
For destination guides, the default order remains:
1. Outdoor places to explore
2. Places to eat
3. Places to stay

Affiliate lodging or booking links belong primarily in the stay/planning portion, not before the reader has received useful destination information.

## Gear Recommendations
When recommending gear:
- Explain the use case.
- Mention important limitations.
- Avoid claiming a product prevents injury or guarantees safety.
- Prefer categories and practical criteria over giant shopping lists.
- Update discontinued or broken links.

## Stay22 / Travel Integration
When Stay22 or another travel-booking integration is active:
- Verify the integration loads correctly.
- Confirm outbound links resolve to the intended destination.
- Avoid duplicate or competing booking widgets on the same page unless there is a clear reason.
- Keep the editorial recommendation distinct from automated monetization layers.

## Newsletter
Affiliate offers may be included when relevant to the issue topic.

Rules:
- The newsletter must still be useful without the affiliate link.
- Do not overload an issue with unrelated offers.
- Keep disclosure visible.
- Track clicks separately from editorial links where possible.

## AHA
Member discounts or partner offers must be clearly distinguished from editorial recommendations.

Do not imply an advertiser, affiliate, or partner has special safety approval from AHA unless that is factually and contractually true.

## Technical Check
Before publication:
- Test the destination URL.
- Confirm HTTPS.
- Verify affiliate parameters remain intact.
- Check mobile behavior.
- Confirm no redirect loop.
- Confirm required rel attributes where appropriate.
- Ensure link does not break Sage or internal navigation tracking.

## Review Cadence
At least quarterly, review:
- Broken links
- Expired programs
- Changed commission relationships
- Discontinued products
- Poor-performing placements
- Pages with excessive monetization

## Metrics
Track where available:
- Affiliate clicks
- Click-through rate
- Conversion rate
- Revenue per click
- Revenue by page
- Revenue by partner
- Refund/cancellation rate where reported

Do not optimize solely for click volume. A high-click placement that damages trust is a bad placement.

## Quality Check
A monetized page is ready when:
- The page answers the reader's question first.
- The affiliate recommendation is relevant.
- Disclosure is clear.
- The destination URL works.
- The page does not feel dominated by sales links.
- Safety/editorial claims remain independent.

## Success Standard
Affiliate revenue should feel like a useful extension of trip planning, not a toll booth between the reader and the answer.
