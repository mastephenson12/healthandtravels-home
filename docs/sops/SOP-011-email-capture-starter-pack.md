# SOP 011 — Email Capture + Arizona Family Adventure Starter Pack

## Purpose
Turn anonymous Health & Travels and Sage visitors into subscribers by offering a genuinely useful Arizona family adventure resource, then guide them back into the ecosystem.

## Trigger
A visitor reaches a relevant H&T or Sage page where a free planning resource would improve the experience.

## Owner
Health & Travels Growth/Email Operator.

## Primary Lead Magnet
Arizona Family Adventure Starter Pack.

## Core Principle
Earn the email after providing value. Do not treat the visitor's inbox address like a tollbooth.

## Starter Pack Contents
Maintain a practical, family-first bundle such as:
- Arizona adventure ideas
- Packing checklist
- Heat/sun planning guidance
- Kids activity or treasure hunt
- Arizona coloring/activity page
- Family adventure planner

Contents may evolve, but each item must remain useful and current.

## Placement Rules
Good placements include:
- After a useful section of an H&T article
- Near the end of a destination guide
- On a dedicated subscribe page
- After a Sage planning result
- In newsletter/social campaigns

Avoid:
- Immediate full-screen interruption before the visitor reads anything
- Multiple competing forms on one viewport
- Safety warnings interrupted by promotional forms

## Form Requirements
Collect the minimum information needed.
Default:
- Email address
- First name only if useful for personalization

Clearly explain what the subscriber receives.

## Consent and Trust
- Never pre-check optional marketing consent where prohibited.
- Provide clear unsubscribe mechanisms through the email platform.
- Do not sell or casually share subscriber information.
- Link privacy information where appropriate.

## Delivery Flow
1. Visitor submits email.
2. Validate successful form submission.
3. Add subscriber to the correct Beehiiv audience/segment.
4. Deliver or link the Starter Pack immediately.
5. Show a useful confirmation/thank-you state.
6. Provide the next logical action, usually Explore Arizona or Plan with Sage.

## Welcome Sequence
Recommended sequence:

### Email 1 — Deliver the promised resource
- Starter Pack link/download
- Brief explanation of what H&T does
- One simple next action

### Email 2 — Help plan the first adventure
- Useful Arizona planning guidance
- Link to Sage

### Email 3 — Safety and confidence
- Practical family hiking/safety resource
- Link to relevant H&T guide

### Email 4 — Keep exploring
- Introduce recurring newsletter value
- Introduce AHA only when it naturally fits

Do not turn the welcome sequence into four consecutive sales emails wearing hiking boots.

## Tracking
Use consistent source tags/UTMs so signup source is measurable.
Track:
- Form views
- Form completion rate
- Confirmation success
- Starter Pack delivery/download
- Welcome email opens/clicks
- Sage clicks
- H&T return visits
- AHA conversions downstream
- Unsubscribe rate

## QA Checklist
Before publishing or changing a form:
- Test on Android and desktop.
- Test valid email submission.
- Test obvious invalid email behavior.
- Confirm subscriber reaches Beehiiv.
- Confirm Starter Pack delivery/link works.
- Confirm thank-you state works.
- Confirm privacy/unsubscribe expectations are clear.
- Confirm tracking parameters.
- Confirm the form does not block primary page content.

## Optimization
Test one meaningful variable at a time, such as:
- Headline
- Placement
- CTA wording
- Starter Pack description
- Inline vs end-of-article form

Judge changes by qualified signup and downstream engagement, not merely form clicks.

## Success Standard
Visitors understand the value exchange, receive the promised resource immediately, and have a natural path back to Health & Travels, Sage, and eventually Arizona Hikers Association.
