# SOP 014 — Arizona Hikers Association Community Engagement and Retention

## Purpose
Create a repeatable system that gives AHA members ongoing reasons to participate, return, and renew.

## Trigger
Use continuously after a member completes onboarding.

## Core Principle
Retention should come from recurring value, not from hoping people forget to cancel.

## Weekly Engagement Rhythm
Aim for a simple, sustainable cadence.

### Monday — Adventure Prompt
Share a trail, destination, seasonal idea, or planning prompt.

### Midweek — Community Conversation
Ask a useful question that encourages members to contribute experience, photos, or local knowledge.

### Weekend — Get Outside
Highlight an outing idea, challenge, event, safety reminder, or Sage planning prompt.

Do not post merely to satisfy a calendar. Empty engagement theater is still empty.

## Monthly Value Rhythm
Each month should include a mix of:
- new or refreshed trail resources
- seasonal safety guidance
- local trip ideas
- member discussions
- community recognition
- meetup/event opportunities where available
- Sage planning prompts
- member-only or member-first resources where genuinely offered

## Content Categories
Rotate among:
1. trail discovery
2. family hiking
3. safety and conditions
4. local knowledge
5. community stories
6. challenges and goals
7. events/meetups
8. gear/packing education
9. Sage planning
10. member feedback

## Member Participation Rules
Encourage members to:
- introduce themselves
- post trail reports
- share original photos
- ask questions
- recommend appropriate trails
- participate in events and challenges
- help newer members

Do not pressure members to disclose personal information.

## Safety and Moderation
Community advice must not override official closures, weather warnings, fire restrictions, land-manager rules, or emergency guidance.

Remove or correct content that materially encourages unsafe or illegal behavior.

For hikes/events, communicate:
- expected difficulty
- approximate distance/elevation when known
- weather considerations
- water/shade concerns
- family suitability
- meeting details
- cancellation/change process

## Events and Meetups
Before promoting an event:
1. verify location and access
2. verify current closures/restrictions
3. define difficulty and intended audience
4. identify meeting location/time
5. communicate what participants should bring
6. provide cancellation/weather procedure
7. avoid presenting informal community outings as professional guiding unless properly structured and insured for that purpose

## Community Recognition
Highlight positive member contributions such as:
- useful trail reports
- helping another member
- consistent participation
- family adventure milestones
- challenge completion

Recognition should reward contribution, not turn the community into a popularity contest with hiking boots.

## Re-Engagement
For members who become inactive:
- send a useful new resource
- highlight an upcoming activity or seasonal opportunity
- recommend a Sage plan
- ask for preferences/feedback when appropriate

Avoid guilt-based messages.

## Renewal Support
Before renewal, members should already have experienced value.

Where communications are used, emphasize:
- what is currently available
- recent useful additions
- upcoming community opportunities
- how to manage membership

Do not hide cancellation or renewal terms.

## Feedback Loop
Regularly collect member feedback on:
- desired trail regions
- family vs adult-only interests
- event preferences
- resources they actually use
- barriers to participation
- Sage usefulness

Use feedback to change programming rather than ceremonially storing it in a spreadsheet.

## Metrics
Review monthly:
- active members
- weekly/monthly participation
- posts/comments/reactions where available
- event participation
- resource clicks/downloads
- Sage referrals
- monthly retention
- annual renewal
- cancellations
- stated cancellation reasons

## At-Risk Signals
Investigate when:
- onboarding participation falls
- community activity trends downward
- event attendance collapses
- cancellations spike
- members repeatedly report access/email problems

## Quality Standard
A member should be able to answer:
1. What did AHA help me do this month?
2. What can I do next?
3. Why is staying a member useful?

If those answers are unclear, add value before adding more promotion.

## Next Steps
Use results from this SOP to inform content planning, AHA events, Sage improvements, newsletter topics, and membership offer changes.
