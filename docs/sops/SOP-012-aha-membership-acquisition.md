# SOP 012 — Arizona Hikers Association Membership Acquisition

## Purpose
Create a consistent, ethical process for turning qualified Health & Travels and Sage users into Arizona Hikers Association members.

## Trigger
Use this SOP whenever creating or improving a membership offer, CTA, landing page, campaign, or handoff from Health & Travels, Sage, Beehiiv, or social content.

## Core Principle
Do not sell membership before demonstrating usefulness. The visitor should understand what AHA helps them do and why ongoing membership is valuable.

## Current Offer
Arizona Family Trail Pass
- $7/month
- $69/year

## Primary Acquisition Sources
- Health & Travels destination and safety pages
- Sage trip-planner results and itinerary pages
- Beehiiv newsletter
- Starter Pack follow-up
- Social media
- YouTube
- Community referrals

## Standard Customer Journey
1. Discover useful Arizona content.
2. Plan an outing with Sage.
3. Receive continued value through newsletter or downloadable resources.
4. Encounter AHA when community, events, trail resources, or ongoing support are relevant.
5. Visit the AHA join page.
6. Choose monthly or annual membership.
7. Enter onboarding SOP.

## Offer Positioning
Health & Travels helps people discover.
Sage helps people plan.
Arizona Hikers Association helps people belong and keep exploring.

Membership messaging should emphasize outcomes such as:
- finding more Arizona trail ideas
- meeting other Arizona hikers
- family-friendly outdoor connection
- community events and meetups
- trail packs and useful resources
- staying active and exploring more often

Avoid vague claims, artificial scarcity, or benefits that do not yet exist.

## CTA Placement Rules
Place an AHA CTA where it naturally follows the user's intent.

Good locations:
- near the end of an H&T guide after the user has received value
- after a successful Sage plan or itinerary
- in relevant newsletter sections
- in Starter Pack follow-up emails
- on dedicated community/membership pages

Do not overload every paragraph with a join button.

## Standard CTA Language
Use concise, benefit-led language such as:
- Join Arizona Hikers Association
- Join the Arizona hiking community
- Keep exploring with AHA
- Get the Arizona Family Trail Pass

## Landing Page Requirements
The membership page should clearly communicate:
- who AHA is for
- what members receive
- current monthly and annual pricing
- cancellation/renewal expectations
- what happens immediately after joining
- community access instructions
- support/contact path

## Trust Requirements
Before publishing a claim, confirm it is true today.
Do not advertise events, discounts, resources, or partner benefits until they are actually available.

## Tracking
Use UTM parameters for acquisition links where practical.
Track:
- source
- page/campaign
- join-page visits
- checkout starts
- completed memberships
- monthly vs annual plan

## Quality Check
Before a campaign goes live, confirm:
- pricing is correct
- checkout links work on desktop and Android/mobile
- no broken redirects
- membership benefits match reality
- analytics links are tagged
- Sage and H&T branding remain clear
- privacy and payment expectations are understandable

## Metrics
Review at least monthly:
- join-page sessions
- visitor-to-checkout rate
- checkout completion rate
- monthly vs annual mix
- cost per acquired member if paid traffic is used
- source-level conversion rate
- first-30-day retention
