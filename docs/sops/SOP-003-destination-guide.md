# SOP 003 — Destination Guide

## Purpose
Create useful, family-first Arizona destination guides that help visitors decide where to go, what to do, where to eat, where to stay, and what to plan next.

## Trigger
A new Arizona destination, town, park, trail hub, weekend area, or family adventure is approved for publication.

## Owner
Health & Travels Content Builder.

## Inputs
- Destination or area
- Primary search intent / keyword
- Family audience
- Available firsthand photos
- Current official information
- Related Sage destination or planning route
- Related Health & Travels guides

## Standard Content Order
1. Outdoor places to explore
2. Family-fit information
3. Safety and seasonal considerations
4. Places to eat
5. Places to stay
6. Suggested itinerary or trip structure
7. Sage planning CTA
8. Related Health & Travels content
9. Arizona Hikers Association CTA when relevant

## Required Research
Verify current details from authoritative sources when relevant:
- Access and hours
- Fees or permits
- Trail status
- Closures
- Parking
- Restrooms
- Water
- Weather or seasonal hazards
- Pet rules
- Reservation requirements

Do not rely on stale summaries for operational details.

## Family-Fit Requirements
Include practical information whenever available:
- Best ages
- Walking or hiking difficulty
- Stroller suitability
- Shade
- Bathrooms
- Water access
- Drive time
- Elevation
- Heat exposure
- Crowds
- Backup plan

## Firsthand Content
If Health & Travels has original photos or firsthand experience:
- Use them prominently
- Label them accurately
- Do not imply firsthand experience where none exists
- Preserve image rights and credit metadata

## Writing Standard
The guide should be practical, specific, and useful before promotional.

Avoid generic travel filler.

Answer the questions a parent would realistically ask before loading everyone into the car.

## SEO Checklist
- Unique title tag
- Unique meta description
- Canonical URL
- One clear H1
- Logical H2/H3 structure
- Internal links
- Descriptive image alt text
- Article or destination-appropriate structured data
- Clean URL slug

## Sage Integration
Every destination guide should include a logical next step to Sage when a matching planning experience exists.

Preferred CTA concept:
**Plan this adventure with Sage**

Use tracked links.

## AHA Integration
Add Arizona Hikers Association only where it naturally extends the visitor journey.

Examples:
- Hiking destination
- Trail community content
- Repeated outdoor participation
- Meetup relevance

Do not force a membership pitch into every guide.

## Quality Check
Before publication verify:
- Facts are current
- Links work
- Mobile layout works
- Images load
- No misleading firsthand claims
- Sage CTA works
- AHA CTA works if included
- No obvious duplicate content
- Spelling and grammar are clean

## Output
A live Health & Travels destination guide.
