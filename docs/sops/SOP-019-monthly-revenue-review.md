# SOP 019 — Monthly Revenue Review

## Purpose
Review whether Health & Travels, Sage, the newsletter, affiliate content, and Arizona Hikers Association are producing revenue or moving meaningfully toward it.

## Trigger
Once per month, preferably in the first week of the new month.

## Core Questions
1. What made money this month?
2. What moved closest to making money?
3. What created activity but little business value?
4. Where did users drop off between discovery and revenue?
5. What should we stop, continue, improve, or test next month?

## Monthly Review
- Total site sessions and organic clicks/impressions
- New newsletter subscribers
- Sage visits, starts, and completions if tracked
- AHA join-page visits, checkout starts, memberships sold, monthly/annual mix, and churn
- Affiliate clicks and revenue
- Ad/sponsor revenue if active
- Total monthly revenue

## Content Review
Identify:
- top pages by traffic
- top pages by conversion assistance
- pages with impressions but weak CTR
- pages with traffic but weak CTAs
- pages worth updating, internally linking, or monetizing more intentionally

## Funnel Review
Review movement through:
**Health & Travels → Sage → Newsletter/AHA → Revenue**

Find the biggest bottleneck and the fastest practical improvement.

## Decision Rule
Choose only 3–5 priorities for the next month. The review must produce decisions, not merely a respectable pile of numbers.

## Metrics Over Time
- total monthly revenue
- recurring membership revenue
- affiliate revenue trend
- revenue per 1,000 sessions
- subscriber growth rate
- membership conversion rate
- funnel progression from H&T → Sage → AHA
