# SOP 020 — Social Content Repurposing

## Purpose
Turn strong Health & Travels and Arizona Hikers Association content into multiple social assets that drive discovery, trust, and repeat traffic without recreating everything from scratch.

## Trigger
- new page publishes
- newsletter sends
- timely safety topic becomes relevant
- event or meetup needs promotion
- high-performing evergreen page deserves renewed distribution

## Channels
- Facebook
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram / Threads when used
- X / Bluesky when used
- Pinterest
- LinkedIn when the topic fits

## Process
1. Select the source asset: destination guide, safety article, checklist, newsletter, member story, event, or photo set.
2. Define the single core takeaway.
3. Choose one CTA: read the guide, plan with Sage, get the Starter Pack, join the newsletter, join AHA, or attend the event.
4. Create platform-specific versions rather than blindly pasting the same caption everywhere.
5. Use approved visuals, prioritizing firsthand photos.
6. Confirm destination URLs and UTM parameters where useful.
7. Log what was posted and review basic performance.

## Minimum Repurposing Package
For each important asset, usually create:
- 1 long-caption social post
- 1 short-form post or video hook
- 1 visual post or Pinterest pin
- 1 newsletter mention/reuse opportunity

## Safety Article Package
- education/warning post
- short video script
- checklist graphic concept
- newsletter tease

## AHA Event Package
- save-the-date post
- reminder post
- day-before reminder
- post-event recap
- member/gallery highlight if available

## Visual Rules
Preferred order:
1. original firsthand photos
2. approved branded graphics
3. approved stock/generated supporting visuals if necessary

Verify rights/privacy, location accuracy, and mobile readability.

## Metrics
- clicks by platform
- engagement by content type
- newsletter signups from social
- Sage visits from social
- AHA visits/joins from social
- saves/shares for evergreen content
