# SOP 009 — Beehiiv Newsletter

## Purpose
Create and publish a useful Health & Travels newsletter that brings readers back to Arizona content, Sage, and Arizona Hikers Association without turning the email into a carnival of competing calls to action.

## Trigger
A scheduled Monday/Friday newsletter or a special safety/seasonal send is due.

## Owner
Health & Travels Content/Newsletter Operator.

## Inputs
- Current H&T article or destination priority
- Current seasonal/safety relevance
- One primary CTA
- Optional Sage or AHA secondary CTA
- First-party or approved image
- Newsletter URL/tracking links

## Standard Structure
1. Subject line
2. Preview text
3. Short opening hook
4. Primary Arizona story or destination
5. Practical planning value
6. One clear primary CTA
7. Optional secondary section: Sage, safety, or AHA
8. Footer/disclosures/unsubscribe handled by Beehiiv

## Content Rules
- Lead with usefulness, not promotion.
- One main idea per send.
- Favor outdoor places first, then food, then stays when destination-focused.
- Use concise paragraphs and mobile-friendly formatting.
- Avoid generic travel filler.
- Never invent current closures, weather, permits, or safety conditions.
- Use current sources for time-sensitive claims.

## CTA Priority
Choose one primary CTA based on reader intent:
- Explore guide
- Plan with Sage
- Download Starter Pack
- Join AHA

Do not present all four as equally urgent.

## Image Standard
- Use approved first-party imagery when available.
- Keep file size reasonable.
- Add descriptive alt text.
- Confirm rights/credit status before use.

## Link Tracking
Use UTMs consistently for H&T, Sage, and AHA links.
Recommended dimensions:
- utm_source=beehiiv
- utm_medium=email
- utm_campaign=<newsletter_topic_or_date>
- utm_content=<specific_link>

## Pre-Send QA
- Subject is specific and not clickbait.
- Preview text adds context rather than repeating subject.
- All links work.
- CTA goes to the correct page.
- Mobile preview is readable.
- Images load and have alt text.
- Safety/current facts were verified when needed.
- No broken personalization tags.

## Post-Send Review
Track:
- Opens
- Click-through rate
- Clicks to H&T
- Clicks to Sage
- Starter Pack signups
- AHA clicks/conversions
- Unsubscribes

## Success Standard
The newsletter consistently helps subscribers choose and plan Arizona adventures while moving interested readers naturally into Sage, email lead magnets, and AHA membership.
