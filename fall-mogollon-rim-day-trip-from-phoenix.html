Fall Mogollon Rim Day Trip From Phoenix

Leave Phoenix in the morning, spend the heart of the day among pines and lake views at Woods Canyon Lake, then eat in Payson before heading home. The schedule, family-fit ratings and route are already built for you.

By Mark Stephenson, RNArizona father and family-adventure publisherUpdated September 7, 2026

The plan in one sentence

Depart Phoenix early, use Woods Canyon Lake as the single outdoor anchor, add one short Rim overlook only if energy and weather allow, eat in Payson on the return drive, and be home before the day turns into a family endurance test.

Best forFamilies wanting forest, water and flexible walking
Kid difficulty2/5 if you keep the shoreline walk short
Shade4/5 around the pine-forest portions
BathroomsYes at developed recreation areas; verify seasonal status

Your plug-and-play schedule

6:30 AM

Leave Phoenix

Fuel the car the night before and start with water and simple breakfast food already packed. Earlier departures create more margin for traffic, parking and afternoon weather changes.

8:00 AM

Optional breakfast reset in Payson

If the crew needs a real breakfast before heading up the Rim, use Payson as the reset point. Keep it short enough that the main outdoor portion of the day still starts early.

9:15 AM

Arrive at Woods Canyon Lake

Shade 4/5Bathrooms: developed areaKid difficulty 2/5Stroller fit: limited

Use the restroom first, then choose a shoreline section rather than assuming the family needs to complete a full loop. Natural surfaces can be rocky, root-filled and uneven.

11:30 AM

Picnic + free exploration

Eat before everyone gets over-hungry, then let the kids explore the forest edge, water views and picnic area. This is intentionally unstructured time.

1:00 PM

Optional Rim overlook

Add a short scenic stop only if the weather is stable and the group still has energy. Skip it without guilt if clouds are building or the family is done. The itinerary works without collecting every possible stop like travel Pokémon.

2:30 PM

Start back toward Payson

Leaving the high country with daylight and margin makes the trip easier, especially as fall weather becomes less predictable.

3:30–4:30 PM

Post-adventure meal in Payson

Suggested stop: Rim Country BBQ and Wood-Fired Pizza on Main Street. Treat the restaurant as a convenience, not a dependency: verify hours before departure and keep a backup option.

5:00 PM

Drive home

The goal is to arrive home feeling like you actually had a day off, not like you completed a federally funded logistics exercise.

30-second departure checklist

  • Check Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest alerts and road access
  • Check the hourly forecast at Woods Canyon Lake elevation
  • Verify current day-use fees and restroom availability
  • Download the Google Maps route for offline use
  • Pack water, lunch/snacks, sun protection and a warm layer
  • Bring shoes that handle roots, rocks and uneven shoreline terrain
  • Set a family turnaround time before walking
  • Skip overlooks or shoreline sections if storms, wind or fatigue change the day

Why this works better than a “10 things to do” list

Families do not need ten more possibilities. They need one outing that fits the day. This playbook deliberately uses one anchor activity, one optional scenic add-on and one meal stop so you can spend less time deciding and more time outside.
