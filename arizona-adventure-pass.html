Good to know

Is this the paid Arizona Family Weekend Pass?

No. The Arizona Adventure Pass is the free wallet-style entry point. The separate Family Weekend Pass is the $7/month Arizona Hikers Association offer for ongoing curated planning and community benefits.

Can I add it to my wallet today?

Not yet. This page is the early-access launch page. Apple Wallet and Google Wallet issuance will turn on after the provider integration is tested.

Will this replace email?

No. Email remains the dependable home base. The wallet pass will be a convenient companion for current adventure links and limited, useful updates.
